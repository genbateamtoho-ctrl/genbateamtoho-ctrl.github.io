Privacy Policy

東邦鋼業株式会社 (the Company) complies with Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information and this policy to protect customers' privacy.

1. Personal information means information that can identify a customer, including names, addresses and phone numbers, and information that identifies a person when combined with other information.

2. We use personal information to ship ordered goods, settle payments, communicate about transactions and shipping, respond to inquiries and perform related activities. Information entered in quote/order forms (name, company, contact details, shipping address and product specifications) is stored in our Supabase database to prepare quotes, communicate about orders and arrange goods. It is retained for 3 years from the order date and then appropriately deleted.

3. We do not provide personal information to third parties without consent except where required by law; necessary to protect life, body or property when consent is difficult to obtain; entrusted to carriers, Supabase or other processors as necessary for the stated purposes; or necessary to cooperate with public authorities or their contractors carrying out statutory duties. The Supabase database is stored and managed in the AWS Tokyo region (ap-northeast-1). Data is handled only in Tokyo, Japan. We enter appropriate contracts with processors and audit them regularly.

4. Security measures: appoint a personal information manager, establish internal rules and conduct regular audits; train staff regularly; control access to areas handling personal information and prevent theft of equipment/media; and control database access, encrypt communications with SSL/TLS and prevent unauthorized access.

5. Cookies and analytics: we use cookies to improve service and convenience. Cookies identify browsers but do not themselves identify individuals. You may refuse cookies through browser settings, although some functions may become unavailable. Google Analytics 4 collects viewed pages, referral sources and device information to understand site usage. See Google's Privacy Policy and Analytics Terms of Service. Tracking can be disabled with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. We do not currently serve ads such as Google AdSense; this policy will be revised and published if ads are introduced.

6. Payment information: credit card payments are not currently offered. If introduced, online payments will be processed securely through a payment service provider without the Company retaining card numbers. This policy will be updated with the provider and handling details.

7. Requests for disclosure, correction, addition, deletion or suspension of use: after verifying the requester's identity, we will respond without delay as required by law. Contact the office below. A copy of official identification such as a driver's license or passport may be requested. There is no fee for disclosure requests.

8. Contact: 東邦鋼業株式会社. Personal information manager: the Company's representative director. Email: info@tetsuko.co.jp. Phone: +81-6-6981-7226. Hours: weekdays 9:00–17:00 Japan time, excluding weekends, public holidays and year-end/New Year holidays.

9. Policy changes: we may revise this policy when necessary. Revisions take effect when published on this website.

10. Established: November 6, 2026.
